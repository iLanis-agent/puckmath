🏒 PuckMath

A team can win four straight while getting outshot every night, and the standings will not tell you it is borrowed time. PuckMath computes the numbers that separate skill from streak: save percentage, shot share, and PDO - the hockey luck meter that always comes back to collect.

  • Goaltending - save percentage written the hockey way (.912, not 91.2%), plus goals-against average per 60 minutes.
  • Shot share - Corsi (all attempts) and Fenwick (unblocked attempts). Possession hockey has a number, and this is it.
  • PDO, the luck meter - shooting % plus save %. The whole league regresses to 100; a team at 102.3 is riding a wave that gravity always collects.
  • Points pace - 2 points a win, 1 for the overtime loss, scaled to an 82-game season. The honest projection of the current streak.
